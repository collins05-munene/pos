Frequently Asked Questions

Does OmniPOS work on hardware I already own?

Yes! OmniPOS runs on iPads, Android tablets, Windows PCs, and Mac. It connects seamlessly via Bluetooth/USB to standard thermal receipt printers, barcode scanners, and cash drawers.

What happens if my internet connection goes down?

Our Smart Offline Engine keeps taking payments and recording orders offline. Once your network reconnects, data automatically syncs to the cloud safely.

Can I upgrade or cancel my plan anytime?

Absolutely. You can switch between plans or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your account settings with zero cancellation penalties.
